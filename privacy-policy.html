Privacy Policy – Browser App

ZmrAppStudio · Effective date: November 13, 2026

Browser App is a lightweight in-app browser (WebView container). It displays web content inside the app. The page it opens is set by us through a link stored in Firebase Realtime Database, so the content can be updated without releasing a new app version. This policy explains what is handled and how your privacy is protected.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or sell your personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Firebase

On start, the app reads one small configuration value (the link to open, an optional backup link and an on/off message) from Firebase Realtime Database. This read does not send any personal information from you. The last received link is saved on your device so the app can open it if the connection fails.

Web Content

The pages you view are loaded from the internet and are operated by their owners. Those pages may use cookies, local storage or other technologies under their own privacy policies. Cookies and site data are kept in the app’s WebView storage on your device and are removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load web pages and detect when you are offline.
  • Files you choose: if a web page asks you to pick a file or photo, Android’s file picker is shown and only the file you select is shared with that page.

Downloads

Files you choose to download are saved to your device’s Downloads folder. They are not sent to us.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. The app refuses pages with invalid security certificates.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with an updated date.
