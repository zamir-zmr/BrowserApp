Privacy Policy – Browser App

ZmrAppStudio · Effective date: November 14, 2026

Browser App is a simple web browser. This policy explains what is stored and how your privacy is protected.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell your personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Data Stored on Your Device

To work like a normal browser, the app saves the following only on your own device (in the app’s local storage): your open tabs, browsing history, search history, bookmarks, shortcuts, settings and the name you may enter in Settings. Each person’s data is separate and is never uploaded. You can delete any of it at any time using History, Delete browsing data or by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app. Pages opened in Incognito tabs are not saved to history.

Web Content

The pages you visit are loaded from the internet and are operated by their owners. Those sites may use cookies, local storage or other technologies under their own privacy policies. Search queries you type are sent to the search engine you choose in Settings (for example Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo or Yahoo), which handles them under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load web pages and detect when you are offline.
  • Microphone (optional): only if you tap the voice-search button and allow it.
  • Files you choose: if a web page asks you to pick a file or photo, Android’s file picker is shown and only the file you select is shared with that page.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Connections use HTTPS where the website supports it. Sites without HTTPS are marked as not secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with an updated date.
